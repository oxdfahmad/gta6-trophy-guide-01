    Six phases to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions. Story first, then the stranger strands, then the activities, then the robberies and money, then the collectible sweep, and finally the long grinds that feed 100% completion. Around 200 hours in total, by the usual launch-era estimates.

      Missables & risks

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be earned after the story, because the open world stays open and there are no online trophies. The real risk is not a trophy you can miss — it is a save you did not make.

      Three habits that protect a playthrough

      1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission. The game has no mission replay, so a spare save is the only way back.
      2. Meditate the moment you find a spot. Do not leave a meditation spot for later — especially Lucia's balcony spot in Chapter 1, which is reported to disappear after that chapter.
      3. Never turn on a cheat before the credits. Cheats disable trophies and block saving, and they only unlock after the story anyway.
